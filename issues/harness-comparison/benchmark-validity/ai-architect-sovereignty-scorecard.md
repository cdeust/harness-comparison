## Observed condition

The charter defines ten mandatory sovereignty dimensions and permits only a
labeled summary maturity band. No current published release scores the complete
five-project AI Architect solution against those dimensions using the new
capstone evidence gates.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

At least one dimension will remain unmeasured, unavailable or below its
preregistered maturity criterion when the complete solution is exercised on the
internal and independent tracks.

## Why it matters

The project objective is a sovereign autonomous system. Isolated feature claims
cannot establish data control, replaceability, critical reasoning or governance
across the complete stack.

## Non-claims

No score is inferred from repository size, test count, issue state or prior
benchmark output. The five internal components are one product population, not
five competitors.

## Reproduction protocol

Preregister an explicit rubric for each charter dimension, including required
observations and missing-data treatment. Execute the complete stack through the
functional, workload, security, provenance and orchestration studies. An
independent reviewer verifies every dimension against release artifacts.

## Acceptance criteria

- All ten dimensions publish their rubric, evidence, missingness, failures and
  uncertainty separately.
- Every numeric value traces to immutable raw data and a reproducible analysis;
  no hidden weighting masks an unavailable or zero dimension.
- Internal results remain separate from Track R generalization, while matched
  external references provide context only for the declared capability.
- The summary maturity band, if used, states its derivation and cannot improve
  when required evidence is missing.
- Fixes link to the affected dossier and pass its smallest regression slice;
  the final release passes independent review and the publication gate.

## Regression obligation

Update only affected dimensions for a local product fix, but rerun the full
scorecard whenever the stack boundary, rubric, protocol or candidate population
changes.
